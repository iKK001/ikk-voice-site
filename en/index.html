Your voice,
spoken clearly

When your own voice is hard to understand: just speak – iKK Voice writes it down and reads your words out clearly. In daily life, in conversation and on the phone.

Coming soon to the App Store – iPhone & Mac Coming soon to Google Play – Android

Four modes for everyday life

You speak as usual. iKK Voice recognises your words, shows them as text and reads them out in a clear, natural voice.

Listen and Speak

Dictate into the text field, correct if needed and have the text read aloud – or copy it.

Phone call

Your sentences collect in a list. One tap and iKK Voice speaks the sentence into the call.

Listen and Suggest

The AI suggests sentences that sum up what you wanted to say. Tap the right one to play it. Needs an OpenAI API key.

Listen and Translate

What you say is translated into a language of your choice, shown and read aloud.

iPhone, Mac and Android – what you need

For iKK Voice to understand you and speak for you, it needs speech recognition and a voice. Where these come from depends on your device.

iPhone & Mac

Ready to go – at no cost

Apple ships speech recognition and voices with the operating system. iKK Voice uses them for free, with no account and no API key.

  • Just install and start speaking
  • Optional: more voices and services with your own API key
Android

With your own API key

Apple's free speech recognition and voices are only available on iPhone and Mac. On Android you therefore need an account and an API key with a cloud service.

  • OpenAI, ElevenLabs, Deepgram or Cartesia – one key covers both speech recognition and voice
  • Voiceitt – speech recognition only, currently in English
  • The provider may charge for usage

Your data stays with you

No account with us, no ads, no tracking. iKK Voice only uses a cloud service after you have explicitly agreed – and you can withdraw that consent at any time.
